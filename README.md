# Refrn Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 November 2026
**Developer:** After Hours
**Contact:** shn.tw9@gmail.com

Refrn is a reading app. It finds phrases in a novel that refer back to something earlier in the book and lets you jump to
that earlier passage. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

## In short

- **No account.** Refrn has no sign-up and no login. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
- **Your books stay yours.** The books you import, your reading progress and your settings stay on your device.
- **Two kinds of data reach us.** Anonymous usage statistics, which help us find bugs and improve the app, and your
  ratings of underlines, but only if you agree to share them. Neither contains your name or the text of your books
  (ratings contain the rated passage; see section 3).
- **No ads, no tracking, no selling.** We do not show ads, track you across other apps or websites, or sell or rent
  your data to anyone. Refrn contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

## 1. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We cannot see it.

- **Your library:** the EPUB books you import, their covers, and which sample books you have hidden.
- **Reading progress:** where you are in each book and which books you have finished.
- **Reading settings:** text size, theme, layout, brightness, and a book's return-to-reading position and recent searches.
- **Underlines:** the phrases Refrn underlines and the earlier passages they point to. They are worked out on your
  device. Nothing about your book is sent to a server to do this.
- **Your ratings** (thumbs up or down on an underline), whether or not you choose to share them.
- **Usage statistics waiting to be sent** (see section 3). They are kept only until they are sent, at most about 1 MB,
  and are excluded from device backups.

Deleting Refrn from your device deletes all of the above.

## 2. Data stored in your own iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Refrn saves the underlines it has worked out for each chapter in **your private iCloud
database**, so another device or a reinstall does not have to work them out again.

- Each saved chapter is named by a code calculated from the book's text, not by its title. It holds the underlined
  phrases, the earlier passages they point to, and their positions in the book.
- This database belongs to your Apple Account. Apple stores it, and we, the developers, cannot read it.
- To delete it, open **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Refrn**, then choose **Delete Data**.

Refrn works fully without iCloud. Everything then stays on the device.

## 3. Data sent to us

Both kinds of data below are stored for us in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). They are labelled with a **random installation
ID** that Refrn creates on your device. It is not your name, email or Apple Account, it is not shared with any other app,
and a new one is made if you delete and reinstall Refrn.

### Usage statistics

To find bugs and improve the app, Refrn sends us small anonymous reports about how the app is used. They are sent
automatically from devices signed in to iCloud. A report contains:

- the random installation ID, the app version, your device model and its iOS version;
- which features you used and when, and how long things took (for example opening a book or turning a page), reading
  time, how often you opened, followed and rated underlines, and whether imports and saving worked;
- crash and performance reports that Apple's MetricKit provides to the app.

Reports **do not** include your name, book titles, authors, passages, file names, what you searched for, or error
messages that could contain your content. We use them only to find bugs, measure how the app performs and improve it.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, nothing is sent; reports wait on the device as described in section 1. To have the
reports we have received deleted, or to object to them, write to us (see section 7).

### Ratings of underlines

When you rate an underline for the first time, Refrn asks whether you want to share your ratings. If you choose
**Share**, each rating is sent to us. It contains:

- your rating (up or down), the book's title, a code calculated from the book's text, and the chapter number;
- the underlined phrase, the sentence it is in, and the earlier passage Refrn pointed to, with their positions;
- what kind of phrase it was, how the app decided on it, and the app and engine versions.

We use ratings only to measure and improve how well Refrn's underlines work.

To stop sharing ratings, or to have the ratings we have received deleted, write to us (see section 7). Taking a rating
back (tapping the same thumb again) deletes its shared copy. If you choose **Don't Share**, your ratings stay on your
device.

### Who can see this data

Usage statistics and shared ratings are readable only by the Refrn team. They are not visible to other users, and we do
not share them with anyone else. Apple stores them for us as part of iCloud.

## 4. Data sent to other services when you use a feature

Some features need the internet. They send only what the feature needs.

- **Sharing a book from Apple Books.** When you share a book from Apple Books to Refrn, the app sends the book's store
  ID and your country to Apple's iTunes Lookup service to get its title and author. It then searches **Project
  Gutenberg** (gutenberg.org) for a free edition, using the title and the author's last name, and downloads the EPUB
  from there. Project Gutenberg's own privacy policy applies to that request.
- **Voice search in the library.** If you use voice search, iOS asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission.
  Your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, which may send the audio to Apple to process it. Apple's
  privacy policy applies. We never receive or store the audio.
- **Opening books from other apps.** When you open an EPUB in Refrn from Files, Safari, Mail or AirDrop, the file is
  copied into the app. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Refrn uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) for the storage described in sections 2 and 3. Apple processes that data under the
[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 5. What we do not do

- We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app does not use the advertising identifier.
- We do not show ads.
- We do not sell, rent or trade your data.
- We do not use your books, ratings or usage statistics to train AI models for anyone else.
- We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data or payment details.

## 6. How long we keep data

- **On your device and in your private iCloud:** until you delete it, delete the app, or delete Refrn's iCloud data.
- **Usage statistics and shared ratings:** no longer than 12 months after we receive them. Then we delete them.

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **Stop sharing ratings** at any time by writing to us. The app keeps working without sharing.
- **Delete your data** on the device and in your private iCloud as described in sections 1 and 2.
- **Ask us** to access or delete the usage statistics and ratings we have received from you, to stop sharing ratings,
  or to object to usage statistics, by writing to shn.tw9@gmail.com. Because they are tied only to a random installation ID, we may need you to
  tell us roughly when you used the app and on which device model so we can find them. We will answer within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights under data-protection
law. We rely on your **consent** for shared ratings, and on our **legitimate interest** in keeping the app working and
improving it for usage statistics, which are anonymous and limited to what is listed above.

Refrn has no account, so there is no account to delete.

## 8. Children

Refrn is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you
believe a child has shared data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

Data sent by Refrn travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data in iCloud is protected by Apple's security. Usage
statistics and shared ratings can be read only by the Refrn team.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the effective date above. If a change means new kinds of
data leave your device, we will tell you in the app before it applies.

## 11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: shn.tw9@gmail.com.
